# Getting Started

## Welcome to customRisk

customRisk is a structured risk management platform that helps your organisation identify, track, review, and respond to risks. It is designed around the concept of a **Risk Register** — a central record of all known risks within a given scope, such as a project, team, or business unit.

This Help section walks you through the key concepts and features of the platform. Whether you are a risk owner, a register administrator, or just getting started, you will find guidance here on how to get the most out of customRisk.

## Navigating the app

Use the left-hand navigation bar to move between sections. The sidebar can be collapsed to icon-only mode to save screen space.

- **Home** — Your personal dashboard. Shows risks that need your attention, overdue reviews, and (for administrators) a system-wide summary.
- **My Risks** — A consolidated view of all risks assigned to you across every register you have access to.
- **Registers** — The list of all risk registers you can access. Select a register to view its risks, configuration, and audit history.
- **Audit (administrators only)** — A system-wide audit log of every change made across all registers — who did what and when.
- **Users (administrators only)** — Manage user accounts, roles, and access. Only System Administrators can access this section.
- **Templates (administrators only)** — Create and manage reusable risk templates that can be applied when setting up new registers. Helps standardise risk entries across the organisation.
- **Help** — This documentation section. Covers all features of customRisk as well as high-level guidance on risk management concepts.

## Your profile

Click your name or profile icon in the top-right header to view and update your profile. From there you can change your display name and password. You can also create and manage API keys for programmatic access.

## Home Dashboard

The **Home** page is your personal dashboard. It is designed to surface the risks that need your attention without requiring you to manually scan every register.

**Risks needing attention**

This panel lists all open risks that are assigned to you where the review is overdue or due within the next 30 days. Each item links directly to that risk — click it to open the risk detail panel in the relevant register without needing to navigate there manually.

**Overdue count summary**

A summary count of how many of your assigned risks are currently overdue for review. This gives you a quick at-a-glance indicator of your review obligations across all registers.

**Recent system events (System Administrators only)**

System Administrators see an additional panel showing the most recent audit events across the entire system — a live view of activity without needing to visit the full Audit log.

> **Note:** If you have no risks that are overdue or due soon, the dashboard will show a positive confirmation message rather than an empty panel. No news is good news.
