## 1. Release Goal

The goal of v1.7.1 is to ensure the in-app Help page contains comprehensive, accurate, and well-structured content covering:

1. Every feature and function of customRisk (user guide content)
2. High-level conceptual training on risk management in general

No screenshots are included in this release. Text-only content. Screenshots will be added in a later release once the UI is finalised.

The Help page already exists (`frontend/src/pages/HelpPage.tsx`) and has a solid initial structure. This release is about filling it out completely, ensuring nothing is missing, and improving the quality and coverage of what is there.

## 5. Content Requirements Per New Section

### The Home Dashboard (Tab 1: Getting Started)
Explain:
- The page a user sees when they first log in
- "Risks needing attention" panel: shows open risks owned by the user where the review is overdue or due soon. Click any risk to navigate directly to it.
- "My overdue risks" summary count: the number of risks that are past their review date
- For System Administrators: there is an additional "Recent system events" panel showing the latest audit events across all registers
- If the user owns no risks or has no overdue reviews, the panel shows a positive confirmation message

### Inherent and Residual Risk — Concepts (Tab 2)
Explain:
- Inherent risk is the level of risk before any controls or mitigations are applied — the "raw" risk
- Residual risk is the level of risk that remains after applying controls and mitigations
- Why this distinction matters: it allows organisations to quantify the effectiveness of their controls and identify where the gap between inherent and residual risk is too small (controls may be ineffective) or where the residual risk is still unacceptably high despite controls
- In customRisk this is an optional mode per register, enabled by the administrator
- When enabled, each risk has separate Likelihood and Impact fields for inherent and residual scoring, each with their own calculated Risk Score and Risk Level

### Governance and Compliance Context (Tab 2)
Explain:
- Risk registers are a core governance tool used by organisations to meet their obligations under a range of standards and regulations
- ISO 31000 is the international standard for risk management — it provides principles and guidelines for identifying, assessing, and treating risk. customRisk is designed to support ISO 31000-aligned practices
- Many sectors have specific regulatory requirements for risk management (e.g. financial services, healthcare, public sector). A risk register is often a required artefact for audits and regulatory reviews
- customRisk's audit trail and review workflow support these requirements by providing evidence of active risk management
- Brief note: customRisk does not assert compliance with any specific regulatory standard — it provides tools that support compliance programmes

### Risk Culture (Tab 2)
Explain:
- Risk culture is the shared values, beliefs, and behaviours within an organisation that shape how risk is identified, discussed, escalated, and managed
- A healthy risk culture means risks are raised openly, without blame, and reviewed honestly
- Tools like customRisk support risk culture by making risk ownership visible, tracking whether reviews happen, and surfacing overdue risks — but culture is built by people, not software
- Brief tip: encourage risk owners to treat reviews as a genuine checkpoint, not a box-ticking exercise

### Editing a Risk (Tab 4)
Explain:
- From the risk table, click a risk to open the detail panel, then click Edit
- All standard fields and custom fields are editable (subject to register permissions)
- Likelihood and Impact changes immediately recalculate the Risk Score and Risk Level
- All edits are recorded in the audit trail — previous values are preserved

### Deleting a Risk (Tab 4)
Explain:
- Only Register Admins and System Administrators can delete risks
- Deletion is permanent — the risk is hard-deleted from the register
- A full snapshot of the risk is preserved in the audit log before deletion
- Consider closing a risk instead of deleting it — closed risks are retained for audit and governance purposes

### The Risk Detail Panel (Tab 4)
Explain:
- Clicking a risk in the register table opens a side panel showing the full risk record
- The panel shows all standard fields, custom fields, the calculated risk level, and the review status
- The Review button is available in this panel for eligible users
- The panel shows the complete review history: each review entry shows who reviewed, when, any comment, and the next review date that was set at the time
- The Edit button opens the full edit form
- Close the panel by clicking outside it or pressing Escape

### Inherent and Residual Risk — In-register Experience (Tab 4)
Explain:
- When a register is configured to use inherent and residual risk, the risk creation and edit form shows separate scoring sections
- Inherent section: Inherent Likelihood, Inherent Impact → Inherent Risk Score, Inherent Risk Level
- Residual section: Residual Likelihood, Residual Impact → Residual Risk Score, Residual Risk Level
- The risk table displays both the Inherent and Residual Risk Levels side by side
- Note: not all registers use this mode — it is optional and configured by the administrator

### Exporting Risks as CSV (Tab 4)
Explain:
- From within a register, use the Export button in the risk table toolbar to download the current view as a CSV file
- The export includes all visible columns, including custom fields
- Filters applied to the table are reflected in the export — only the filtered risks are included
- The CSV can be opened in spreadsheet software for further analysis or reporting

### Configuration Export and Import (Tab 3)
Explain:
- Register Admins can export a register's current published configuration as a JSON file from the Configuration tab
- This JSON file can be imported to create a new template or to replicate the configuration elsewhere
- Importing a configuration file into the Templates section creates a new template — it does not directly import into an existing register
- The exported file contains all fields, scoring model, and settings but no risk data

### Exporting Audit Data (Tab 7)
Explain:
- The audit log (system-wide and per-register) supports CSV export
- The export includes the same events visible in the filtered view — apply filters before exporting to scope the output
- Audit exports are useful for governance reviews, compliance reporting, and external stakeholder briefings
- Audit data cannot be edited before or after export — it reflects the immutable record
